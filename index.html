KING ★
Biggest |GEX| strike on the board. Price's anchor — gravitates toward it, slows around it. The day's reference level.
REVERSAL ↘
Heaviest level on the opposite side of zero from the King. Together they frame the room price moves inside.
GUARD ◆
Any strike carrying ≥50% of the King's weight. Defends against price moves — usually has to be worked through before reaching the King.
GAP ≈
3+ consecutive near-zero strikes. No hedging friction — price travels these stretches quickly in either direction.
